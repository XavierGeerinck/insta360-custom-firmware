# Milestone 3, Workstream I2: Bluetooth audio routing — Plan

> **For agentic workers:** host only. Never run `adb`, never open a device. TDD: a failing test
> first, ALSA behind traits with fakes. Test with `cargo test -p waved-audio --lib`.

**Goal:** phone audio from the Telink module reaches the speaker through the same chain as USB
audio (volume, EQ, limiter, echo-canceller reference); during a call the processed microphone goes
back to the phone; a meeting recorded during a Bluetooth call carries the far end.

**Spec:** `../TODO.md` I2; `docs/BLUETOOTH_PROTOCOL.md` "Audio (I2)" and "Verified on the device
(2026-10-13)"; `docs/AUDIO_HARDWARE.md` (UAC2 routing, the far end of a call).

**Facts this rests on (device, 2026-10-13):** the module's audio is on TDM-A, `hw:0,0`, 48 kHz,
2 ch, S16_LE both ways, the SoC is the clock master; `arecord -D hw:0,0` alone captured the phone's
music with no host command. `BluetoothState::phone_audio` (`0xB2` bit 1) and `call_audio` (bit 2)
are published on `bus.bt` by `waved-bt`.

## Decisions

| ID | Decision | Why | Cost if wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| I2-1 | **USB and Bluetooth mix**; neither takes precedence. Phone audio is a third playback input (`PlaybackService::play_phone`), added to the host's period with the same saturating add as local playback, before the speaker DSP. | macOS keeps the UAC2 stream open and sends zeros when nothing plays, so stock's "the current source keeps the speaker while it is active" would lock the phone out (or need level detection). Mixing has no state, drops nothing, and the echo canceller's reference covers both sources. The user controls each source. | Both play at once (a Mac call and phone music); the fix is a gate in `run_playback_loop` where the two are mixed. |
| I2-2 | The **far end** (`subscribe_far_end`, recordings) is host + phone, still before local playback, tones, DSP and volume. `recording_mix` and `AudioService` need no change. | A Bluetooth call's far end lands in recordings exactly like a USB call's. | Phone music played during a recording is in the recording too, as Mac music already is. |
| I2-3 | **Paths follow the bits:** speaker path (`hw:0,0` capture) open while `phone_audio`; mic path (`hw:0,0` playback) open while `call_audio`. Each thread re-reads `bus.bt` every period (50 ms while closed). | Stock opens and closes its paths on the same bits. | — |
| I2-4 | **Clock switch:** `bt.playback_clock: true` also opens `hw:0,0` playback while `phone_audio` and writes silence outside a call, for the case where TDM-A capture needs the playback side running. Off by default (capture alone worked with `arecord`). | One JSON field to flip on the device. | — |
| I2-5 | The Bluetooth **uplink is the processed mono on both channels**, not the Stereo pair: the same frame and signal as the USB microphone (after AEC, residual echo, voice EQ, make-up gain, NS, mic gain/mute, limiter). | HFP is mono; the module may read one slot only. | A module that sums the slots gets the same level. |
| I2-6 | **Downstream jitter and drift:** the capture is re-chunked into speaker periods; one period of silence is queued when the stream opens (≈ 21 ms, absorbs scheduling jitter); a period is dropped when the phone queue holds `buffer_periods`. TDM-A and TDM-B share the SoC clock, so drops should stay 0. Uplink: the UAC2 uplink's `Cushion` (2 periods) at each (re)start. | Same as the USB bridge; no resampler. | ≈ 21 ms more speaker latency for phone audio, 43 ms for the uplink. |
| I2-7 | **Volume:** phone audio follows the Wave's speaker volume and mute, like USB. The phone's volume reports (`0xD5`) are not mapped. | Stock's mapping is only inferred. | The phone's slider does not change the Wave's level (it may still scale the stream inside the module). |
| I2-8 | **Device handling** like the USB bridge: failures retried every second, logged once until they change (`RepeatFilter`, now shared in `crate::repeat_filter`); xruns counted and recovered with `prepare`; SCHED_FIFO 18; nothing blocks the audio worker (the uplink reads the processed broadcast, the playback loop polls the phone queue). | — | — |
| I2-9 | Config: optional `bt` block in the audio config: `enabled` (true), `device` (`hw:0,0`), `period_frames` (1024), `buffer_periods` (4), `playback_clock` (false). | Same shape as `uac`; 1024 matches the pipeline. | If TDM-A refuses 1024, set 480 (stock's capture period). |

## Tasks

1. **Shared `RepeatFilter`** — move the test with it; `uac::bridge` uses it.
2. **Playback phone input** — tests: a phone period is played and referenced; phone and host periods
   mix; the far end carries host + phone but not local playback; `play_phone` reports a full queue
   as `Full` and a stopped loop as `Stopped`; `phone_queue_depth`.
3. **Paths** — tests: idle → nothing; music → speaker; call → speaker + mic; clock switch opens
   playback with music, mic only in a call.
4. **`PhoneDownstream`** — tests against a fake capture and a fake speaker: nothing opens while the
   bit is clear; opening queues one silent period, then whole periods; a 480-frame capture is
   re-chunked into 1024-frame periods; the bit clearing closes the PCM; a full speaker queue drops
   and counts; xruns are counted and recovered; an open failure retries; a stopped speaker ends
   the loop; stats.
5. **`PhoneUplink`** — tests against a fake playback: closed while not in a call; a call opens it,
   writes the cushion then the processed mono on both channels; the call ending closes it; the
   clock switch writes silence during music; xruns recover and re-cushion; open failures wait
   `retry_frames` before the next try.
6. **Config and stats** — `bt` block parses with defaults and rejects bad values; `bt_audio` in the
   stats JSON only when wired.
7. **ALSA and wiring** — `bt_audio::alsa::spawn`; `main.rs` additive edits.
8. **Docs** — `AUDIO_HARDWARE.md` "Bluetooth audio (I2)"; `BLUETOOTH_PROTOCOL.md` Audio (I2) and the
   implementation outline; `TODO.md` I2 status.

## Device-pass checklist (I2)

Deploy (`docs/DISPLAY_HARDWARE.md#deploy`), phone paired (`BLUETOOTH_PROTOCOL.md`). Read
`/tmp/audio_stats.json` → `bt_audio` and `/tmp/waved.log` (`grep bt-`).

1. **Music, idle Mac.** Play music on the phone. Expect `bt_audio.speaker_active: true` within
   ≈ 1 s of `0xB2` bit 1, `capture_frames` rising ≈ 48 000/s, `speaker_drops` and `capture_xruns`
   0, music audible, and the Wave's volume ring changes its level. Pause: `speaker_active` false
   once bit 1 clears (note how long the module keeps bit 1 after a pause).
2. **If no audio or `open_failures` rises:** read the log line. Then set
   `"bt": {"playback_clock": true}` in `/etc/waved-audio-config.json`, restart waved, repeat 1.
   Note which setting works; if 1024-frame periods are refused, try `"period_frames": 480`.
3. **Echo.** With music on the speaker, `aec.linear_erle_db` should sit near the USB figure (≈ 20 dB
   or better); `aec.reference_rms` > 0 while the phone plays.
4. **Call.** Call the phone, answer on the phone. Expect `mic_active: true`, `playback_frames`
   rising ≈ 48 000/s, `playback_xruns` 0 after the start, the far end audible, and the caller hears
   you clearly without hearing themselves. Mute on the Wave: the caller hears silence. End the
   call: both paths close (`mic_active` false; `speaker_active` false once bit 1 clears).
5. **Both at once.** Mac playing (USB) and phone music: both audible, no crackle, `uac` and
   `bt_audio` drop counters 0. Expected by I2-1.
6. **Recording.** Start a recording during a Bluetooth call, talk both ways, stop. The recording has
   the room and the caller, the caller at a level that does not follow the Wave's volume.
7. **Latency.** Clap near the phone's mic during a call and note the delay heard on the Wave; note
   `hw:0,0` capture/playback `hw_params` (`/proc/asound/card0/pcm0{c,p}/sub0/hw_params`) while open.
8. **CPU.** `top` during a call: the `bt-downstream` / `bt-uplink` threads should be a few percent.
