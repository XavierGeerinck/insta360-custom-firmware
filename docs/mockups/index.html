Wave display redesign

Every screen of the waved touch UI, drawn for the round 400 × 304 panel. Each mockup is placed in real framebuffer pixels, so the numbers on this page can be used directly in lv_obj_set_pos.

The panel is a 380 px circle cropped by a 400 × 304 framebuffer. It is 228 px wide at the top edge and 380 px wide through the middle.

Every tappable object stays within 182 px of the centre, and every target is at least 44 px. Actions that are hard to undo sit away from everything else.

The device sits in the middle of a meeting table, so people read it from about a metre away and from every side.

What changed in revision 2

A second pass focused on how the device is really used: mute and volume many times per meeting, record a few times a day, settings rarely.

  • Tap the dial to muteThe largest target on Home and Recording now does the most frequent job. The ring turns red so the whole table can see it. Home
  • Volume in one tapQuieter and Louder sit left and right on Home and while recording, in the same place. The dial shows the level while it changes. Changing volume
  • Menu moved to the topThe status pill opens the menu. Before, "Menu" sat right under Record, where a slip of the finger started a recording. Menu
  • Safer recording controlsMark, Pause and Stop sit in one row. Stop needs a one-second hold, and a quick tap explains that. Muting while recording says the audio is left out. Holding Stop
  • A clear question after recording"Send this recording?" with Send, Keep and Delete. If nobody answers, the file stays on Wave after a minute instead of the screen waiting forever. After stop
  • Audio modes you can seeAll six modes sit around a live preview. Directional modes are aimed by dragging the shape. Follow talker aims itself. Audio mode
  • Plain words"Pre-roll" is now "includes last 30 s" on the Record button. Errors say what happened and what to do next.
  • One Back button112 × 44 px, bottom centre, on every screen including lists. Lists fade out above it instead of running underneath.
  • A PIN for Files and admin settingsEarlier meetings and the upload server are protected in a shared room. Mute, volume, Record and audio settings never ask for it. PIN
  • Screens that were missingStart-up, first start, a dimmed resting screen, Bluetooth, Mic height, Display, Date and time, and a choice of what happens after each recording.

How the layout works

Six rules, applied the same way on every screen.

The ring is the roomThe dial in the middle of Home and Recording stands for the table. An arc lights up toward whoever is talking. Tapping it mutes. The same ring shows volume, playback, update progress and calls.
Same place, same jobQuieter is always left, Louder always right, mute always in the middle, recording controls always at the bottom. Home and Recording only differ in what the bottom row says.
Bottom centre does the main thingRecord, Stop, Send, Back and Confirm all sit at the bottom centre. Back is the same 112 × 44 px button on every screen.
Status opens the menuThe status pill at the top opens the menu, far from Record. Messages appear in the same spot for a few seconds and never cover a button.
Colour means somethingTeal means live or selected. Red means recording, muted or destructive. Amber means paused or needs attention. Everything else is grey on black, which also hides the edge of the round panel.
No precise taps neededTargets are at least 44 px, main actions at least 50 px. Stop needs a one-second hold and Delete asks first, because a mis-tap there can't be undone.

Start-up

What people see before the first meeting.

Main

Home follows the meeting state on its own. Mute and volume are one tap away, and the dial tells the whole table what the microphones are doing.

Recording

Same layout as Home, so mute and volume don't move. Only the bottom row changes to Mark, Pause and Stop.

Settings

One menu opens from the status pill. Lists use LVGL's circular-scroll pattern (lv_example_scroll_6): the middle row grows and rows near the edge shrink and fade.

Recordings

New screens: today "Files" only shows a placeholder toast.

Connections

Wi-Fi keeps today's QR setup flow. Uploads replaces the Webhook screen and adds the choice of what happens after each recording.

System

Settings that used to be placeholder rows or were missing. Destructive actions confirm first.

Messages

Shown at 1:1 in the status position. One line, at most 230 px wide, with an icon for the severity. Success and info stay 2 s, warnings and errors 4 s. The last two show the status pill while charging and on low battery.
